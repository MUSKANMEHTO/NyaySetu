Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about NyayaSetu and our services

How does AI legal help work?

Our AI legal assistant is trained on comprehensive legal databases and case law. When you ask a question, it analyzes your situation, identifies relevant laws and precedents, and provides clear, actionable guidance in simple language. While the AI provides helpful information, it's not a replacement for professional legal advice in complex matters.

How do I find the right lawyer for my case?

Simply describe your legal issue in our Lawyer Finder tool. Our AI will match you with verified lawyers who specialize in your specific case type. You can then review their profiles, experience, client reviews, and rates before making your choice. All our lawyers are verified and licensed professionals.

Are AI-generated documents legally valid?

Yes! Our document generator creates legally sound documents based on current laws and regulations. However, we recommend having important documents reviewed by a lawyer before signing. We offer an optional legal review service where verified lawyers can check your documents for additional peace of mind.

Is NyayaSetu free to use?

Basic AI legal guidance and access to our Knowledge Hub are completely free. Our Lawyer Finder service is free to browse, but lawyer consultations are paid based on their individual rates. Document generation has a small fee per document, with discounts for bulk usage. We believe in making legal help accessible while ensuring quality service.

How secure is my personal information?

We take data security very seriously. All communications are encrypted end-to-end, and we comply with strict data protection regulations. Your personal information is never shared with third parties without your explicit consent. Lawyer-client confidentiality is maintained for all professional consultations.
